Financing Products

Tailored solutions for every real estate investment strategy

LONG-TERM HOLD

DSCR Loans

Debt Service Coverage Ratio financing for rental properties. Qualify based on property cash flow, not personal income or tax returns.

Key Features:

  • No personal income verification
  • No tax returns required
  • 30-year fixed & ARM options
  • Up to 80% LTV
  • 1-4 unit properties
  • DSCR as low as 1.0

Ideal For: Experienced investors with rental portfolios, self-employed investors, those with complex tax returns.

SHORT-TERM

Fix & Flip Loans

Renovation financing for investors purchasing, rehabbing, and reselling properties. One loan covers acquisition and construction costs.

Key Features:

  • Up to 75% ARV and 100% LTC financing
  • Purchase + rehab in one loan
  • 6-12 month terms
  • Interest-only payments
  • Flexible draw schedules
  • Close in 7-10 days

Ideal For: House flippers, renovation specialists, investors seeking quick-turn opportunities.

TRANSITIONAL

Bridge Loans

Short-term financing to bridge the gap between purchase and permanent financing or property stabilization.

Key Features:

  • Fast closings (7-14 days)
  • Up to 75% LTV
  • 6-24 month terms
  • Interest-only options
  • Flexible underwriting
  • Exit strategy focused

Ideal For: Time-sensitive acquisitions, properties needing stabilization, investors awaiting permanent financing.

DEVELOPMENT

New Construction Loans

Ground-up development financing for residential and light commercial construction projects.

Key Features:

  • Land + construction costs
  • Up to 80% of project cost
  • 12-24 month terms
  • Progress-based draws
  • Single-family & small multifamily
  • Mini-perm options available

Ideal For: Developers, builders, investors creating new rental inventory or spec homes.

INCOME PROPERTY

Multifamily Loans

Financing for 5+ unit properties including small apartment buildings and mixed-use properties.

Key Features:

  • 5+ units: commercial programs
  • Mixed-use properties accepted
  • Up to 80% LTV
  • 30-year fixed available
  • Portfolio discounts
  • Value-add opportunities

Ideal For: Investors scaling into larger properties, cash flow focused strategies.

COMMERCIAL

Commercial Real Estate

Financing for commercial properties including retail, office, industrial, and mixed-use investments.

Key Features:

  • Up to 75% LTV
  • 5, 7, 10-year fixed options
  • 25-30 year amortizations
  • DSCR as low as 1.20
  • Cash-out refinance available
  • Non-recourse options

Ideal For: Investors diversifying into commercial, owner-users, 1031 exchange buyers.

BULK FINANCING

Portfolio Financing

Blanket loans covering multiple properties under one financing package with streamlined management.

Key Features:

  • 2-25 properties in one loan
  • Cross-collateralization
  • Simplified servicing
  • Competitive bulk pricing
  • Flexible release options
  • Mixed property types accepted

Ideal For: Experienced investors with large portfolios seeking operational efficiency.

ASSET-BASED

Hard Money Loans

Asset-based lending focused on property value and equity. Fast funding for unique situations conventional lenders won't touch.

Key Features:

  • Equity-based approval
  • Close in 3-7 days
  • Up to 70% LTV
  • Short-term (6-24 months)
  • Flexible qualification
  • Credit challenged accepted

Ideal For: Distressed properties, foreclosures, urgent situations, credit challenges.

FLEXIBLE FUNDING

Line of Credit

Revolving credit line for fix & flip and new construction projects. Draw funds as needed and only pay interest on what you use.

Key Features:

  • Draw funds as needed
  • Pay interest only on balance used
  • Reusable credit line
  • Perfect for multiple projects
  • Fast access to capital
  • Flexible repayment terms

Ideal For: Active flippers, serial developers, investors managing multiple renovation or construction projects simultaneously.
